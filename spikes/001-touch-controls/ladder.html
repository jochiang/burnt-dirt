OPPONENT LADDER

Drives the real game in an off-screen iframe and measures each rung against fixed seeds. The claim being tested is that the ladder is ordered by competence rather than by miss radius — so the test is not "does a Spoiler miss less", it is "does a Spoiler's error stop depending on the wind, while a Tosser's keeps growing with it". A Spoiler that merely had a smaller error constant would fail that column.
